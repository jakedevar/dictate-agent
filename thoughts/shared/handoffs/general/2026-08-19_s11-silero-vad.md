# S11 — Silero VAD handoff

## Delivered

- New `dictate-vad` crate using CPU-capable `voice_activity_detector` 0.2.1
  (embedded Silero v5 ONNX model), fixed to 16 kHz/512-sample frames.
- Configurable probability threshold, minimum speech, trim padding,
  trailing-silence duration, and snapshot polling interval.
- Completed captures are gated before STT. A no-speech verdict records real
  VAD work, skips STT/injection as `no_speech_detected`, and emits the usual
  empty final transcript.
- Speech captures are trimmed to the padded first/last Silero speech frames.
- `one_shot` and `wake_word` use an additive `AudioSource::snapshot` seam to
  auto-stop after speech followed by configured trailing silence. Toggle/PTT
  does not snapshot or poll.

## Measured fixture result

On the committed 33.27 s speech fixture, the CPU VAD pass measured **113.23
ms** and reduced STT input to **31.34 s** (1.93 s / 5.8% trimmed). This is an
in-process fixture result, not a microphone or GPU end-to-end latency claim.

## Fixture provenance

`speech.wav` derives from the MIT-licensed upstream VAD test asset; its exact
provenance is in `crates/dictate-vad/tests/fixtures/NOTICE.md`. Silence and
quiet-tone fixtures are generated 16 kHz mono WAVs.
